# Research-Quality Proof Harness

This harness is a minimal validity note for two fixed cases on cleaned HEP research surfaces. It is not a benchmark, a scorecard, or a general eval platform.

What this harness proves:

- on these fixed fixtures, weak evidence can force abstention (theoretical-conflict surface)
- on these fixed fixtures, missing semantic authority fails closed under strong claim-word temptation (critical-questions surface)
- the single-run canonical trace for those bounded cases is locked
- the rubric wiring rejects a trace that keeps a fail-closed status but breaks a case invariant, a keyword-driven classification made without semantic sampling, and a relation claimed from weak evidence

What this harness does not prove:

- retrieval coverage
- provenance (same-work) matching: the cases that covered it exercised a sampling-based same-work matcher that was removed on 2026-10-12; same-work identification is now done by the agent with the citation-triangulation and deep-literature-review skills
- taxonomy quality
- coverage-style scoring
- benchmark breadth
- end-to-end research quality across tasks
- robustness under model drift or broader stochasticity
- variance bounds
- open-ended semantic correctness beyond these bounded sampled-contract cases

Why the selected cases are not local capability proxies:

- each case passes only by refusing to overclaim
- local convenience signals do not pass on their own: claim-word strength or weak title-only evidence
- the harness is intentionally about bounded judgment validity, not about how broadly the system can retrieve, classify, or cover a domain

Minimal contamination boundary:

- contamination posture is intentionally narrow: fixed in-repo fixtures, low-risk interpretation, and no benchmark-superiority claim
- contamination is only a boundary note here; this lane does not introduce a contamination-audit subsystem
- low contamination risk does not upgrade this harness into evidence about general capability or product readiness
- proof-bearing baseline authority lives in the fixed aggregate proof metrics; `timestamp` and `aggregateOutcome.resource_overhead` are retained only as incidental runtime bookkeeping and are not authoritative evidence

This lane must not be used to justify:

- broader eval platform or productization work
- front-door expansion
- retrieval, taxonomy, or coverage metrics
- fixture growth for "completeness"
- trace-schema work, contamination-audit expansion, variance analysis, or naming cleanup under this harness
