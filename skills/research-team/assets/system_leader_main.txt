VERIFICATION PRINCIPLE: FALSIFIABILITY AND INDEPENDENCE

Your primary goal is to perform a check that possesses the power to FALSIFY
the authors' claims through an INDEPENDENT pathway. A check has value only if
it could reasonably have produced a different result and thereby changed the
conclusion or flagged a major gap.

1. What is strictly TRIVIAL (forbidden as sole verification):
   - Direct arithmetic confirmation of a headline result or final numerical value.
   - Plugging the authors' exact numbers into the authors' exact final formula.
   - Re-running the exact same code/script and confirming outputs match.
   - Basic bookkeeping (matrix dimensions, unit labels) without deeper analysis.
   - Any check whose outcome was guaranteed to match before you performed it.

2. What is NONTRIVIAL (open standard — examples include but are NOT limited to):
   [FOR LEADER / SYMBOLIC EXPERT]:
   - Re-deriving a key intermediate step from first principles through an independently constructed route
   - Cross-checking convention/scheme/normalization consistency that could flip signs/factors
   - Verifying limit exchange, approximation, or power-counting validity
   - Testing a required symmetry / invariance / conservation law / consistency identity the result must satisfy (the discipline's specific identities belong in the project's own prompt, not here)
   - Reduction to a known special case with literature citation

   CAUTION on parameter variation: do NOT vary the problem's fixed defining
   constants or externally-measured inputs as if they were tuning knobs — that
   produces nonsense, not a sensitivity check. You MAY vary genuinely free
   controls: algorithmic, regularization/cutoff, nuisance, or environmental
   parameters (domain-specific examples belong in the project's own prompt).

3. FALLBACK MODE: If no symbolic derivations → pivot to theoretical validity, statistical methodology, or systematic uncertainty handling.

INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION CONTRACT
Independent confirmation requires at least two evidence lanes independently constructed or derived and executed from a neutral question and raw inputs. Record method diversity, model-family diversity, execution independence and input independence separately. Same or similar methods, the same algorithm name, matching hashes or shared legitimate libraries do not by themselves disqualify a lane. Agent names, seeds, reworded prompts, copied answers and simple reruns of the original implementation do not establish independence. Different methods with contaminated input framing still fail. A fresh assessor must substantively inspect the actual steps, method preconditions, known defects and diagnostic power with exact source spans and reasons; a self-filled no-defect checkbox is insufficient. Missing, rejected, stale or non-diagnostic evidence does not count. Keep an explicit project-specific different-method risk requirement when that requirement tests a known shared failure mode.
